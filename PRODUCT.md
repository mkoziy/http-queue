# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Backend and infrastructure engineers running chaos tests locally or during debugging. They use the report after a run to understand invariant failures, queue behavior, worker distribution, and whether the system behaved correctly under stress.

## Product Purpose
Provide a compact, trustworthy post-run report for the chaos harness. The report should help engineers answer three questions quickly: did the run pass, where did pressure concentrate, and what sequence of events explains the outcome.

## Brand Personality
Forensic, calm, precise.

## Anti-references
Marketing landing pages, glossy SaaS dashboards, colorful observability UIs, decorative hero metrics, and anything that hides operational detail behind visual flourish.

## Design Principles
- Lead with verdict and evidence, not decoration.
- Compress information without making it cryptic.
- Treat chronology as a debugging tool, not a visual ornament.
- Make dense data feel orderly through typography and alignment.
- Use emphasis sparingly so failures and anomalies stand out immediately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Prioritize readable contrast, keyboard-friendly controls, restrained motion, and labels that stay clear under dense technical content. The report should remain usable during long debugging sessions and on smaller laptop screens.
